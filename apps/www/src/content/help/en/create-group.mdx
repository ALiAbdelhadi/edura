A group (a class) is what ties everything together: a teacher, a subject, a grade, a hall, a schedule and a price. Without one there is no attendance and no money.

## The steps

1. Open **Classes** in the dashboard and click **Create class**.
2. Pick the **subject** and the **grade**.
3. Pick the **teacher** — they must already have joined your center.
4. Pick the **hall**.
5. Set the weekly **schedule**, with a start and end date.
6. Set the **price**.

<Shot caption="The create-class form" />

## Schedule and clashes

On save, the system generates the individual sessions from the weekly schedule up to the end date. Before it saves, it checks that:

- the hall is not already booked by another class at that time
- the teacher does not already have another class at that time

If there's a clash, the save is refused and you're told exactly where. Nothing is saved half-way.

## Price

There are two plans, and you can enable one or both:

- **Monthly** — the student pays for a month and renews by paying again (nothing is auto-charged)
- **Per session** — the student pays for a single lesson

At least **one plan must be enabled**, otherwise the student has nothing to pay for.

## The hall's rate freezes here

This is the most important line on this page: **the hall's rate is recorded onto the class the moment it is created**. If you change the hall's pricing later, this class keeps settling at the old rate, and the change shows up on new classes only.

> That is intentional. Today's price change must not re-price a class that has been running for two months.
