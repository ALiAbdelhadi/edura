Your wallet balance is your money. A payout request is how it comes out.

## Where

**Wallet** in your dashboard (teacher or center) → **Request payout**.

<Shot caption="The wallet screen and the payout button" />

## Available is not the same as balance

The figure you can request is:

> **Available = wallet balance − open requests**

So if your balance is 5,000 and you have a request under review for 2,000, only 3,000 is available. The amount is reserved the moment you request it, so the same money can never be requested twice.

## The amount

You can request:

- **the whole available balance** — the default if you leave the amount empty
- **a specific amount** below the available figure

## The stages

1. **Under review** — the request is recorded and the amount reserved
2. **Approved** — the accountant has reviewed and approved it
3. **Paid** — the money has actually gone out

You can follow the state from the same wallet screen at any time.

## No minimum, no deduction

There is no minimum request and no withdrawal fee. What is in your wallet is what reaches you.

## Why is my balance lower than I expected?

Every wallet entry names the session and the student behind it. Open the wallet history and each movement shows its source. If the figure still doesn't add up, the usual reason is a session that hasn't been closed yet — distribution happens [when the session closes](/help/close-session-settlement), not when the student pays.
