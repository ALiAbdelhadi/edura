---
title: The guardian email and the adult-messaging lock
summary: Why some students can't message their teachers, and how to unlock it in one step.
---

If a student of yours can't find a way to message their teacher, this is almost certainly why — and it isn't a fault.

## The rule: minor by default

Any student who has **not been shown** to be an adult is treated as a minor. "Not shown" means: no date of birth on file, or one on file that hasn't been verified, or a verified one under 18.

That is deliberate. When a child is involved, the safe assumption is the correct one.

## What is locked, exactly

A minor with **no email channel for a guardian** has messaging with adults locked:

- the teacher
- the assistant
- center management
- center staff

## What keeps working

**Everything else.** Paid classes, materials, grades, the wallet, signing in, attendance — all of it. The account is **never suspended** by this rule.

## How to unlock it

Either of these is enough:

1. **An approved parent link** with an email on file — the strongest, because it's a verified relationship
2. **Adding a guardian email** to the student's details from settings

<Shot caption="The guardian email field on a student's details" />

> **A phone number is not enough.** Email is the only guardian channel we can actually deliver on today, so it is the only one that lifts the lock. The number is stored for the record and for future channels.

## If a student registered at the counter with no email

Then no email is sent at all, and the screen hands you a **registration link** you can copy and give straight to the guardian standing in front of you. The student also sees a notice on their dashboard asking for a guardian email.

## The deadline escalates, it doesn't punish

A grace period starts the moment we learn a minor has no guardian email. During it, reminders arrive and a dismissable notice appears. After it, the notice becomes **non-dismissable** and the center is notified.

Passing the deadline **never suspends the account** and never unlocks messaging. It only makes the matter impossible to forget.

## Parent oversight

An approved parent can read their minor child's threads with adults, **read-only**, without touching read receipts or unread counters. Both sides see a permanent notice that oversight is active — there is no hidden monitoring.
