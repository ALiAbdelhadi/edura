---
title: Checking students in at the counter
summary: Search by student code or QR, pick the session, and take cash and attendance in one step.
---

The counter is the first point of contact when a student walks in. This screen is built so staff can finish in seconds and the queue keeps moving.

<Shot caption="The counter check-in screen" />

## 1. Find the student

From **Check-in** in the center dashboard (or from a receptionist account) you can search by:

- **Student code** — fastest, and the student knows it
- **Name** or **mobile number**
- **QR**, if the student has a card

Typing the code manually always works, even when no camera is available.

## 2. Pick the session

You'll see the student's sessions for today. Pick the one they're here for. If there's only one, it's already selected.

## 3. Already subscribed — done

If they hold coverage for that session, attendance is recorded and that's it.

## 4. Not subscribed — cash and attendance in one step

This is the most common situation in an Egyptian center: the student is standing in front of you with the lesson's money. You don't need a second screen:

1. Pick the plan (single session or a month)
2. Take the cash
3. Record the payment and the attendance together

A numbered receipt is issued, and the payment is written to the ledger immediately — with exactly the same entries as an online payment.

## 5. The counter doesn't always mean "present"

That depends on the teacher's attendance mode:

- **Single check-in** — the counter check-in *is* the attendance. Done.
- **Two-stage** — a counter check-in on its own is **not** attendance. A second check-in at the hall door is required.

In two-stage mode, a student who checked in at the counter but never entered the hall stays in a "counter only" state that is plainly visible to the teacher, and that lesson **does not settle** for them.

Read [the second check-in at the hall door](/help/door-checkin) if your teachers use that mode.
