Creating a center account takes minutes, and the wizard saves as you go — if you close the tab halfway, your answers are waiting when you come back.

<Shot caption="Sign-up screen — choosing a role" />

## 1. Choose "Center" at sign-up

On the **Create account** page, choose **Center**. The role you pick here decides the shape of the dashboard you'll work in afterwards, so pick carefully.

Once you've entered your email and a password, the wizard starts immediately.

## 2. Center details

This step holds the fields students and parents will see:

- **Center name**, as you want it to appear in search results
- **Governorate** and **area** — these are what people search by
- **Address** and a **contact** number or WhatsApp
- **Description** — a line or two on who the center serves

None of this is final. You can edit it any time from **Settings** inside the dashboard.

## 3. The stages and grades you serve

Pick the school stages and grades your center teaches. This choice filters the subjects you'll be offered when creating groups, so pick accurately — though adding or removing later is no problem.

## 4. Halls

Add your halls: name, capacity, and a photo if you have one. A hall is not just a label — it is what prevents schedule clashes once you start timetabling, and it is what the center's share of a lesson is calculated against.

<Shot caption="The halls step in the sign-up wizard" />

> You can finish sign-up with a single hall and add the rest later from **Halls** in the dashboard.

## 5. Done — you land on "Today"

The first screen you'll see is **Today**, and it isn't just numbers: it is the queue of decisions waiting for you — enrolment requests, overdue fees, and today's sessions.

The natural next step is [inviting your first teacher](/help/invite-teacher).
