From **Settings** in the menu. Everything about your account is here.

## Your details

At the top are your **name** and **phone number**. Change what you need and press **Save**.

**Your phone number matters**: it's how the centre finds you at the counter when you don't have your card.

## Guardian email

If you see a notice asking for your guardian's email, that's **the most important thing on this page**. Without it, messaging teachers and centre staff stays locked and your [student ID card](/help/student-id-card) won't be issued.

Enter the email and save. Edura then sends a **confirmation message** to that address to check it works; if it doesn't arrive, press **Resend the link**.

> **It has to actually be your guardian's address, not yours.** It can't be the same as your sign-in email. Every address you enter is recorded and the centre is notified of it.

## Student ID card

A small section with a **Show card** button. The card isn't loaded automatically — it appears when you ask for it. See [Your student ID card and code](/help/student-id-card).

## Password

1. Enter your **current password**
2. Enter the **new one**, then **confirm it**
3. Press **Change password**

The rules are written under the field: **at least 8 characters, including a letter and a digit**. If the current one is wrong you'll see **"Current password is incorrect"**, and if the confirmation doesn't match, **"The two passwords do not match"**.

> **When you change it, every device signed into your account is signed out immediately** — including the one you're on. That's deliberate: if someone had access to your account, they lose it at that moment.

## Devices and sessions

A list of every device currently signed into your account, each with its **last activity**, and the one you're using marked **"This device"**.

- **Sign out** next to a device — signs out that one only
- **Sign out all other devices** — signs out everything except the one you're on

**Check this list if you suspect someone got into your account**, press **Sign out all other devices**, and change your password afterwards.

## Your personal data

Two things here:

### Export my data

Press **Download file** and a file with all your data in Edura is prepared for you.

### Delete account

Press **Request account deletion** and write a **Reason**. The request is reviewed by the platform team rather than acted on immediately, and until it is you can **Withdraw request**.

Two things to know:

- **Your financial records are not deleted.** Payments and receipts remain, because those are records the centre is required to keep. What's deleted is your personal information.
- **If you're under 18**, the request is reviewed with your guardian.

## Things you might run into

**"This account has no password yet"** — you signed in via an invite link and never set one. See [Joining with a centre invite](/help/student-claim-invite).

**Saving your details failed** — check your connection and try again.

**You were signed out suddenly** — most likely the password was changed, or someone used **Sign out all other devices**. Sign in again with the new password.
