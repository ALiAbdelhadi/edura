Your student card is your identity at the centre. Counter staff find you with it in a second instead of spelling your name out.

From **My ID** in the menu, or from [Settings](/help/student-settings-security).

<Shot src="/help/student-id-card-en.webp" caption="The student ID card — the code and the QR square" />

## Your code

An **eight-character** code like `G7K3M2QF`. Memorise it or photograph it; it's what you tell the staff.

- **The first letter** is your education system — `G` General, `A` Azhari, `B` Egyptian Baccalaureate.
- **The last character** is a check character. If staff type a wrong character the system **rejects the code** rather than pulling up a different student by mistake. That's why saying it exactly matters.
- **A leading `X`** means you haven't chosen your education system yet. The code works fine, and it settles as soon as you finish [choosing your system and grade](/help/student-onboarding-track).

**Your code never changes** — not when you move up a grade, and not if you lose the card and get a new one.

## The QR square

The square on the card is readable by a phone camera. It holds a signed link, and the page it opens says whether the card is **genuine**.

**There's no personal information inside the square** — no date of birth, no phone number, no school. If you lose the card and someone finds it, all they can see is a shortened version of your name; they can't get into your account or find out where you are.

## Printing the card

1. Press **Print**
2. Choose a printer, or **"Save as PDF"**

The card is exactly bank-card size, so you can cut it out and keep it in your wallet.

## Sending it on WhatsApp

Press **Share card** — the card becomes a picture you can send. On a phone the share sheet opens straight away so you can pick WhatsApp.

## If the card doesn't appear: "your record is incomplete"

Edura won't issue a card to a student whose record is incomplete. You'll see a list of what's missing instead:

- **Your name** — Fix it in [Settings](/help/student-settings-security)
- **Your phone number** — Add it in Settings
- **Guardian email** — Enter it in the notice on the same screen
- **Your grade and system** — Finish [the wizard](/help/student-onboarding-track)

Once it's complete, refresh the page and it appears on its own.

> **Important:** not having the card **doesn't block anything else**. You can still attend, still pay cash, and staff can still find you by name or phone number. The only thing you're missing is a printed card.

## Things you might run into

**You lost the card** — contact the centre or support. The old card is cancelled and a new one issued, and **your code stays the same**, so your attendance and money are unaffected.

**The card still won't show after completing everything** — refresh the page.

**My parent wants the card** — if your parent is linked to your account and the link is approved, they can view and print it from their own account. See [Family and your parent](/help/student-family).
