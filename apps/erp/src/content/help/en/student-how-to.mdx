Every recipe here starts from your **home screen** after signing in. For more detail, follow the link at the end of each one.

## Subjects and subscribing

### How do I subscribe to a teacher?

1. In the menu press **Subjects**
2. Tap the subject to expand it
3. Pick the group whose times suit you
4. Press **Subscribe monthly**, or choose a lesson and press **Book session**
5. Complete the payment

[More](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe)

### How do I book just one lesson?

1. **Subjects** → open the subject → pick the group
2. In the **lesson picker**, choose the lesson by date and time
3. Press **Book session**

**Pick the lesson before pressing the button**, or nothing happens.

### How do I find a teacher who isn't in my subjects?

1. **Subjects** → the **Browse all** tab
2. Type the subject name in the **search box**
3. Open it and look for the teacher's name

## Money

### How do I top up my wallet?

1. In the menu press **Wallet**
2. Press **Top up** at the top
3. Enter the amount in pounds and confirm
4. Complete the payment

[More](/help/student-wallet)

### How do I pay from my wallet instead of a card?

1. **Subjects** → pick the group → press the subscribe button
2. At the payment step choose **pay from wallet**

If your balance is short the whole thing is refused and no part is taken. Top up the difference first.

### How do I get a receipt for a payment?

1. **Wallet** → scroll to the bottom, to the **Receipts** section
2. Press **Download** next to the receipt

[More](/help/student-receipts)

### How do I print a receipt or save it as a PDF?

1. Open the receipt from **Wallet**
2. Press **Print**
3. Choose a printer, or **"Save as PDF"**

## Attendance and grades

### How do I see my next class?

Open **Home**. The first thing at the top is **up next**, with your next lesson's subject and time.

### How do I see a particular day's schedule?

1. In the menu press **Schedule**
2. Tap the day in the strip at the top
3. Tap the lesson to see the room and the teacher

[More](/help/student-home-and-schedule)

### How do I check my attendance rate?

On **Home**, the **attendance rate** tile. Tap it for the detail behind it.

### How do I see my grade in a subject?

1. In the menu press **Grades**
2. Find the subject in the list — the score and band are there

[More](/help/student-grades)

### How do I download my teacher's handout?

1. In the menu press **Materials**
2. Press **Download** next to the file

## Card and account

### How do I get my student card and print it?

1. In the menu press **My ID**
2. Press **Show my card**
3. Press **Print**, or **Save as image** to send it on WhatsApp

[More](/help/student-id-card)

### How do I link my parent to my account?

1. In the menu press **Family Code**
2. Give your **family code** to your parent
3. When their request arrives, press **Approve**

[More](/help/student-family)

### How do I unlock messaging my teacher?

1. On **Home** or in **Settings** you'll see a notice asking for a guardian email
2. Enter **your guardian's email** and save

Messaging unlocks immediately. A phone number alone isn't enough. [More](/help/student-messages)

### How do I change my password?

1. In the menu press **Settings**
2. In the password section: current, new, then confirm
3. Press **Change password**

**Every device signed into your account is signed out**, including the one you're on. [More](/help/student-settings-security)

### How do I kick someone out of my account?

1. **Settings** → the **devices and sessions** section
2. Press **Sign out** next to the device, or **Sign out all other devices**
3. Change your password afterwards
