Your parent can be linked to your account, which lets them see your attendance and grades and pay for you. This page controls that. From **Family Code** in the menu.

**No link happens without approval.** Every request has to be approved by one of you.

<Shot src="/help/student-family-en.webp" caption="The family screen — your family code and link requests" />

## Your family code

At the top of the page is a code that belongs to you. Your parent uses it from their own account to send you a link request.

**Give the code to your parent only.** Anyone who has it can send you a request.

### Changing the code

If you think the code reached someone it shouldn't have, press **Change code**. A new one is generated and **the old one stops working immediately**. People already linked to you aren't affected.

## Two ways to link

### Your parent sends to you

They use the code to send a request. It appears in your requests table as **pending**, marked as started by the **parent**.

You get two buttons: **Approve** or **Reject**.

### You send to them

1. Enter your **parent's email** or **phone number** in the field
2. Press send
3. The request goes to them, and they approve it from their side

## Link requests

- **Parent** — Their name
- **Status** — Pending / approved / rejected
- **Started by** — Them or you
- **Date** — When the request was made
- **Actions** — Approve or reject — only shown on requests still pending

## What your parent sees once approved

- Your attendance, grades and schedule
- Your [student ID card](/help/student-id-card)
- They can **pay for you** — your subscriptions, from their own money

### And what they see of your messages

If you're under 18, your parent can **read** your conversations with adults (teachers and centre staff) — **read only; they can't write in them and can't delete anything**. A clear notice appears in the conversation for both sides saying a parent is watching, so nobody is monitored without knowing.

**Your conversations with other students are not included.** See [Messages](/help/student-messages).

## Things you might run into

**"Unknown" where the parent's name should be** — their account hasn't been completed yet.

**You rejected a request by mistake** — ask your parent to send a new one, or send one to them by email.

**Sending the request failed** — check the email is right, and that your parent has an Edura account **as a parent**.

**You want to unlink** — there's no button for that here. Contact support.
