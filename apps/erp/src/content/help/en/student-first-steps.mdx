Welcome to Edura. This account is where your schedule, grades, attendance, money and student ID card all live. This page walks you through it from the moment you open the platform.

Edura runs in your browser on a phone or a laptop — there's nothing to install. You can also add it to your phone's home screen so it opens like an app.

<Shot src="/help/student-first-steps-en.webp" caption="The create-account page" />

## 1. Open the sign-up page

At the top of the page tap **Sign in**, then **Create a new account**, and choose **Student**.

## 2. Fill in your details

- **Name** — use the name your school knows you by; it's what your teacher sees and what gets printed on your ID card.
- **Email** — this is what you sign in with every time. Type it carefully.
- **Phone number** — important: this is how the centre finds you at the counter.
- **Password** — at least **8 characters, including a letter and a number**. If something's missing you'll see the message under the field straight away, before you continue.
- **Confirm password** — type it again exactly.

Then press **Create account**.

## 3. Your account works immediately

Students don't wait for approval. The moment you press the button your account is **active** and you go straight to choosing your grade and track.

> The "your account is under review" message is for teachers and centres — you won't see it as a student.

## 4. If your centre already registered you

This is very common: you went to the centre, paid cash, and a staff member created an account with your phone number. In that case:

Use the **same email or the same phone number** the centre has on file. Edura recognises you as the same student and **activates your existing account instead of creating a second one** — so the attendance and payments already recorded stay with you.

If you have an **invite link** from the centre, read [Joining with a centre invite](/help/student-claim-invite) — that route is easier.

## Things you might run into

**"Email or phone already in use"** — there's an **active** account with those details. Try [signing in](/login) instead, and use **Forgot password** if you can't remember it.

**Forgot your password** — on the sign-in page press **Forgot password**, enter your email, and you'll get a link to change it. When you do, every device signed in as you is signed out and has to sign in again — that's deliberate, for your safety.

**Signed in but there are no subjects** — that's normal if you haven't picked your grade yet. Finish [choosing your grade and track](/help/student-onboarding-track).
