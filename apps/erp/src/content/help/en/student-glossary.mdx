If you hit a word you don't know in the platform, look it up here.

## The basics

**Education system** — General, Azhari, or Egyptian Baccalaureate. You choose it when you open your account, and it decides the first letter of [your code](/help/student-id-card).

**Stage** — Primary, Preparatory or Secondary.

**Grade** — your exact year, like second preparatory.

**Track** — the specialisation within a grade, like "Science" and "Literary". Not every grade has tracks.

**Subject** — Maths, Arabic, Chemistry… assigned to you automatically from your grade and track.

## Studying

**Group** — the set of students studying one subject with one teacher at fixed times. This is what you subscribe to.

**Lesson** — a single class on a specific day and time. A group has many lessons.

**Centre** — the place where you take lessons. Some teachers work independently, with no centre.

**Assistant** — an account that acts on a teacher's behalf: recording attendance, replying to messages. Treat them as you would your teacher.

**Materials** — the handouts and files your teacher uploads for your group. [More](/help/student-materials).

## Money

**Subscription** — what makes your lessons paid for. Two kinds: **monthly**, covering the month, and **single lesson**, for one specific lesson. [More](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe).

**Wallet** — your balance inside Edura. You top it up and pay from it. [More](/help/student-wallet).

**Top-up** — putting money into your wallet.

**Receipt** — your proof of payment, with a serial number. [More](/help/student-receipts).

**Refund** — money returned to you, for instance when a lesson is cancelled.

**Reference** — the number on each movement in your wallet record. Quote it to the centre if you have a query.

## Attendance

**Counter check-in** — being checked in by the receptionist when you arrive at the centre.

**Door check-in** — a second check-in at the hall door. Some teachers require it, and **without it your attendance isn't counted** with them. [More](/help/student-attendance).

**Late** — you attended, but after the lesson started.

**Attendance rate** — the share of your lessons you've attended.

## Grades

**Entry** — one grade recorded for you by a teacher (an exam, homework, a quiz).

**Score** — your average in a subject as a percentage.

**Band** — the label for that percentage: excellent, very good, good, pass, weak. **The pass mark is 50%**. [More](/help/student-grades).

## Your account

**Student code** — the 8 characters that identify you at the centre, like `G7K3M2QF`. It **never changes**.

**QR square** — the square on your card; a camera reads it and it proves the card is genuine. It holds no personal information about you.

**Family code** — the code you give your parent so they can link their account to yours. [More](/help/student-family).

**Parent** — your mother, father or guardian, with an account linked to yours after approval.

**Guardian email** — your guardian's email on file with us. **Without it, messaging adults stays locked** and your card isn't issued.

**Session** — a device currently signed into your account. You can end one from [Settings](/help/student-settings-security).

**Block** — stopping someone from messaging you. It works in both directions.

**Report** — telling the platform team there's a problem in a conversation. [More](/help/student-messages).
