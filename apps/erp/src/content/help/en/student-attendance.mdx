This page shows how much you've attended and how much you've missed. From **Attendance** in the menu.

<Shot src="/help/student-attendance-en.webp" caption="The attendance screen — summary and the day map" />

## How your attendance is recorded

You don't record it yourself in the app — it happens at the centre:

### One check-in

You go to the **counter**, give your code or hand over your card, and staff check you in. **That's it, you're present.**

### Two check-ins

Some teachers work differently: a check-in at the counter, then **a second check-in at the hall door** before you go in.

> **This matters:** with two check-ins, the counter alone is **not attendance**. If you check in at the counter and then don't go into the hall, the lesson isn't counted as attended and your teacher sees that. **You have to check in at the door.**

Ask your teacher or the counter staff which way they work, the first time.

## The summary at the top

Three numbers:

- **Present** (green) — Lessons you attended
- **Late** (amber) — You attended, but after the start
- **Absent** (red) — Lessons you missed

## Recent sessions

Right under the summary is a list of the most recent sessions something was recorded for: **the subject's name**, the day and date, and how you were marked. This is what answers "I was at that lesson — why does it say absent?" — the map below it is coloured squares, and names no subject.

## The day map

Below the summary are squares, one per day, coloured by what happened: **green** present, **amber** late, **red** absent. It lets you see your consistency across months at a glance.

A day with more than one lesson takes **its worst status** — so if you attended one and missed another, the day shows red.

## Your attendance rate

The figure on [Home](/help/student-home-and-schedule) labelled **Attendance Rate** is calculated from these numbers. Tapping it on Home brings you here.

## Things you might run into

**"No attendance record"** — you haven't attended a lesson yet, or haven't joined a group.

**You attended but the lesson isn't showing** — wait a little and refresh; it takes until the teacher closes the lesson. If it still doesn't appear, contact the centre with the **lesson's date**.

**Marked absent when you were there** — talk to your teacher or the centre. They're the ones who can correct attendance, not you.

**You checked in at the counter and it wasn't counted** — most likely your group uses **two check-ins** and you didn't check in at the hall door. Next time, check in twice.

## What attendance unlocks

Attendance opens one thing: **reviewing your teacher**. You can't review a teacher until real attendance with them has been recorded. See [Reviewing your teachers](/help/student-reviews).
