title: Reviewing your teachers
summary: Who you can review, how to write one, and the window for editing it.
---

You can give a teacher 1 to 5 stars and write what you think. From **Reviews** in the menu.

<Shot src="/help/student-reviews-en.webp" caption="The reviews screen — Eligible Teachers and My Reviews" />

## Who you can review

The **Eligible Teachers** section at the top lists the teachers you can review right now. A teacher appears there on two conditions:

1. **Real attendance with them has been recorded** in a lesson. Being subscribed isn't enough — you have to have attended.
2. **You haven't reviewed them before** — one review per teacher, from you.

If the section is empty, either you haven't attended yet or you've reviewed everyone.

> If your group uses **two check-ins** and you only checked in at the counter, not at the hall door, that doesn't count as attendance — and the teacher won't appear here. See [Attendance](/help/student-attendance).

## Writing a review

1. Press **Write Review** next to the teacher's name
2. Choose **1 to 5 stars** — **this is required**; without it nothing saves and you'll be asked for a rating
3. Write your **comment** — optional, you can leave it empty
4. Press save

Once saved the review appears in **My Reviews** below, and the teacher's name leaves the available list.

## Editing a review

In **My Reviews**, press **Edit** next to it. You can change the stars or the comment.

> **You have 24 hours only.** A day after you write it, editing closes and you'll see a message saying edits are allowed within 24 hours. So take a moment before you save.

## My Reviews

A table of everything you've written: the **teacher**, the **rating** in stars, the **comment**, the **date**, and an edit button while you're still within the window. If you left no comment it shows **"no comment"**.

## Things you might run into

**The Eligible Teachers section is empty** — you haven't attended a lesson yet, or you've reviewed all your teachers.

**You want to delete a review** — there's no delete button. Edit it while you're within the 24 hours, or contact support.

**You want to review the same teacher again** — not possible; one review per teacher. Edit the existing one instead.
