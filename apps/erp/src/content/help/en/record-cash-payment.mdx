---
title: Recording a cash payment and issuing a receipt
summary: Cash at the counter goes into the same ledger as online — same entries, same numbered receipt.
---

Cash is how most Egyptian centers actually get paid, so it is not a second-class path in Edura. A cash payment is written to **the same ledger** with the same entries as an online one.

## The steps

1. Open **Payments** in the center dashboard (or from a receptionist account).
2. Pick the **student**.
3. Pick the **class** and the **plan** — a month or a single session.
4. Check the amount.
5. Click **Record payment**.

<Shot caption="The record-cash-payment form" />

## The amount is not typed in

The amount comes from **the class's own settings**, not from an open field the operator fills. That is deliberate: a hand-typed amount means the gap between what was collected and what was recorded rests on whoever is standing at the screen.

## The receipt

Every payment gets a **numbered receipt** in the center's own sequence. The sequence is contiguous and never reused, so receipts stand up as a record.

## What happens behind it

A single payment writes:

- the payment row, with its reference and receipt number
- a ledger entry in the same transaction (so both are written, or neither is)
- loyalty points for the student
- the subscription or coverage the payment was for

> **Important:** paying is not distributing. Money reaches the teacher and the center when **the session is closed**, not at the moment of payment — except for an independent teacher. See [closing a session and how money is split](/help/close-session-settlement).

## Refunds

A refund reverses the entries rather than deleting them, and a payment can never be refunded twice — that is enforced in the database itself, not in the screen.
