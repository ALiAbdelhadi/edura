This is where your teacher puts handouts, notes and any files for your group. From **Materials** in the menu.

<Shot src="/help/student-materials-en.webp" caption="The materials screen — your group's file list" />

## Downloading a file

1. Find the file in the list
2. Press **Download** next to it
3. It saves to your device and opens normally

On a phone it goes to your **Downloads** folder, or opens straight away depending on your browser.

## What you can see

Only the files for **groups you're subscribed to**. If a subscription ends, that group's materials may disappear from your list.

## Things you might run into

**"No materials"** — your teacher hasn't uploaded anything. This page is filled by them.

**The download failed** — try again and check your connection. If it keeps failing, contact your teacher; they may have removed the file.

**Your teacher says they uploaded something and you can't find it** — check you're subscribed to the **same group** they uploaded to, and refresh the page.

**It downloaded but won't open** — you probably need an app for that file type (a PDF reader, for instance). Install one and try again.
