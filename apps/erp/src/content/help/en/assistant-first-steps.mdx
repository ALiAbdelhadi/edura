Your account was created by a teacher's invitation, and you act **on their behalf**. At the top of every screen is a line reading "acting on behalf of …" — that is not decoration, it is the scope of your work.

<Shot caption="The delegation line at the top of the screen" />

## You are not signed in as the teacher

You are signed in **as yourself**. You message students and parents under your own name, and every change you make is written to the audit log as "you, on behalf of the teacher".

Your work is traceable and attributed to you. That protects you as much as it protects the teacher.

## Your day

The screen that opens first is **Messages** — because that is the job: you are the first point of contact for the teacher's students and parents, and the teacher steps in for escalations only.

From the bar:

| Button | What you do there |
|---|---|
| Messages | Reply to students and parents |
| Attendance | Take the register, and close the lesson |
| Door | Confirm students at the classroom door |
| Groups | See the teacher's groups and their lessons |
| Gradebook | Enter marks |

The rest is under **More**: handouts, notifications, team notes, and practice.

## The door is your job

If the teacher works in **counter + door** mode, the second confirmation at the classroom door is **yours**. The teacher's account is only a fallback.

The student must have passed the counter first. If you try to confirm someone who has not, the screen says so plainly.

## Closing a lesson moves money

You **can** close a lesson, and that is deliberate. But know that:

- the distribution is written once and **cannot be reversed**
- it is recorded **in your name**, on behalf of the teacher
- it credits the teacher, centre and platform shares immediately

So read the figures on the card before you press confirm.

## What is closed to you

Not a fault and not a missing permission — these are deliberate limits:

- **The teacher's wallet and payout requests** — there is no "Wallet" in your menu at all
- **Group prices** — you cannot create or price a group
- **Adding or removing other assistants**

If you need any of those, the teacher does it.

## Practise first

Under **More**, open **Practice**. It is a complete copy with imaginary data — you can take a register, close a lesson and try the door without touching anything real.
