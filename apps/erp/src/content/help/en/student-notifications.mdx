Notifications tell you what happened: your subscription activated, a grade was recorded, a payment went through. From **Notifications** in the menu.

<Shot src="/help/student-notifications-en.webp" caption="The notifications screen — unread and read" />

## Reading them

Each notification has its text and, underneath, **how long ago** it was — "just now", "5 minutes ago", "3 hours ago", "2 days ago".

Unread ones stand out, and a counter at the top says **how many you haven't read**.

## Tap a notification

Most notifications **take you to the screen they're about** — a grade notification goes to Grades, a payment one to your Wallet. Just tap it.

## Mark all as read

If they've piled up, press **Mark all as read** at the top. The counter goes to zero and the notifications stay there for you to go back to.

## Phone notifications

To get alerts on your phone even when Edura is closed:

1. Open [**Settings**](/help/student-settings-security)
2. Find the **push notifications** section
3. Press **Enable**
4. Your browser asks for permission — press **Allow**

Want them off? Same place, press **Disable**.

### If you denied permission by mistake

You'll see that permission is denied and the button won't work — that's your browser's setting, not Edura's. Open the site settings in your browser, allow notifications, then come back and enable them.

**Tip:** add Edura to your phone's home screen first; notifications work better that way.

## Things you might run into

**"No notifications"** — nothing has happened yet that warrants one. Normal at the start.

**Enabling failed** — check that notifications are allowed in your browser and that you're online, then try again.

**Notifications aren't reaching your phone** — check they're **enabled** in Settings and that your phone isn't on Do Not Disturb.
