---
title: Joining with a centre invite
summary: If the centre registered you at the counter — the link you received, and setting your password for the first time.
---

If you went to the centre and registered there, a staff member has already created your account. You should receive — by email, by WhatsApp, or handed to you at the counter — an **invite link** that lets you take ownership of that account and sign in.

That account already has your details and your student code, so don't create a new one.

<Shot src="/help/student-claim-invite-en.webp" caption="The Secure-your-account screen after opening an invite link" />

## 1. Open the link

Tap the link you were sent. You'll see **"Signing you in…"** for a moment, then the **Secure your account** screen with an email field and a password field.

## 2. Enter your email if you're asked for one

- **If the centre has your email** — the field is filled in and you can't change it. That's deliberate: the invite was sent to that address specifically.
- **If the centre only recorded your phone number** — you'll see an empty email field. Type your email; that's what you'll sign in with from now on.

## 3. Choose a password

Enter a password of **8+ characters**, then press **Save & continue**. The button stays off until the password is long enough.

You go straight to your dashboard.

## Important: the link opens once

As soon as you set your password the **link stops working**. That's so nobody else can use the same link to get into your account. From now on you sign in normally from the **Sign in** page with your email and password.

## Things you might run into

**"This link is no longer valid"** — one of three reasons:

1. The link was already used — try [signing in](/login) with the email and password you set, and use **Forgot password** if you can't remember it.
2. The link expired — ask the centre to send a new one.
3. The link was copied incompletely — if you copied it from a message, make sure you got the whole line, right to the end.

**No link ever arrived** — ask the centre to send one. If they don't have your email, you can instead register normally with the **same phone number** they have on file, and Edura will connect you to your existing account by itself — see [Create your account and sign in](/help/student-first-steps).

**The link opened but there's no email field** — that means the centre already has your email. Just set the password.
