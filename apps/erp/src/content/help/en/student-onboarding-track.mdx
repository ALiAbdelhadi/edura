title: Choose your system, grade and track
summary: The wizard that decides your subjects for you, and reviewing your choice before you confirm.
---

Once your account exists, Edura asks which education system and grade you're in. From those answers it **works out your subjects for you** — you never pick them one by one.

The wizard goes step by step, and a counter at the top tells you which step you're on out of how many.

<Shot src="/help/student-onboarding-track-en.webp" caption="The first step — choosing your education system" />

## 1. Education system

Tap your system: **General**, **Azhari**, or **Egyptian Baccalaureate**.

> If the system you picked only has one stage, the wizard skips the stage step and takes you straight to the grade. That's expected.

## 2. Stage

Choose **Primary**, **Preparatory** or **Secondary**. (Azhari names them "Azhari Preparatory" and so on, and the Baccalaureate has a single stage.)

## 3. Grade

Choose your exact grade — "Preparatory 2", "Secondary 3", and so on.

## 4. Track

This step **doesn't appear for everyone** — only if your grade has tracks — **Science**, **Math** and **Literary** in general secondary, or Baccalaureate paths like **Medicine & Life Sciences**. Pick yours; it's what decides your subjects.

## 5. Second language

Also optional, and only shown if your stage studies one. Pick the language (French, German, Italian… whatever's offered).

If you haven't decided yet, press **Skip** at the bottom — you can sort it out later.

## 6. Review and confirm

The last screen shows all your choices in a table, and underneath it **the list of subjects you'll be given**. Read it before you confirm.

- **If the list has subjects** — check them and press confirm.
- **If the list is empty** — you'll see an amber warning saying this choice assigns no subjects. Press **Back** and change the grade or track, because if you confirm anyway your Subjects screen will stay empty.

## Two things that make this easier

**You can go back any time** — the back button at the top returns you to the previous step without losing what you picked.

**Your choices save themselves** — if you close the page or lose connection, you'll come back to the same step with the same selections.

## Next

After confirming you land on your dashboard. Start with [Your home screen and schedule](/help/student-home-and-schedule), then [Subjects and subscribing](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe) to join a teacher or centre.
