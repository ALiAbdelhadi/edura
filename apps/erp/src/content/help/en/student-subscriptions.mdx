A monthly subscription in Edura **ends on its own**, and nobody renews it for you. This screen is where you see every subscription you have with the date it ends.

<Shot src="/help/student-subscriptions-en.webp" caption="My subscriptions — what is running, and when each one ends" />

## How to get there

Tap **More** at the bottom on a phone, then **Subscriptions**. On a computer it is in the menu under **Money**, above the wallet.

## What you'll find

### Running now

The subscriptions still covering you. Each line shows:

- **the group's name**
- **the plan** — monthly, or a single session
- **the end date** and how many days are left
- **its state** — active, ending soon, or awaiting payment
- **what was paid** — the amount at the time you subscribed, not today's price

### Past subscriptions

The ones that ended or were cancelled. They stay on record for you.

## What the states mean

- **Active** — more than a week left.
- **Ending soon** — a week or less. Start thinking about renewing.
- **Ended** — it has expired. You cannot attend on it, and the counter will tell you so.
- **Awaiting payment** — you started subscribing and never finished paying. It does not cover you until you do.

## The "single session" plan

That's a ticket for one dated session. It carries no day counter, because it does not cover a period — it covers the one session you chose.

## How to renew

Tap **Renew** beside the subscription. It takes you to [Subjects](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe), where you pick the same group and pay again — by card or from your [wallet](/help/student-wallet).

**Renewal is not automatic.** Edura never charges your card on its own; you pay each time.

## The notice on your home screen

If a subscription is about to end, or ended recently, you get a notice about it on [Home](/help/student-home-and-schedule) without going looking. It clears itself as soon as you renew.

## Things you might hit

**The page is empty** — you haven't subscribed to a group yet. Start at [Subjects](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe).

**Mine ended and I didn't notice** — that is what the home notice is for. If it isn't reaching you, turn on [phone notifications](/help/student-notifications).

**I paid and it still says "awaiting payment"** — wait a minute and refresh. If it stays, talk to the centre with your [receipt](/help/student-receipts).
