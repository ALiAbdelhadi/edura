This is the page where you find a teacher and subscribe. From **Subjects** in the menu.

<Shot src="/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe-en.webp" caption="The Subjects screen — My subjects and the available groups" />

## The two tabs at the top

- **My subjects** — opens by default and holds **only your track's subjects**, the ones the platform assigned from your choices in the wizard.
- **Browse all** — every subject on the platform, with a **search box** to find one by name.

Tap any subject to expand it and see the groups available in it.

### The mark beside a subject's name

A subject you're subscribed to carries a mark saying where it stands: **Active**, **Ending soon**, or **Ended**. So you can tell what you're paying for without opening each one. The detail and the renew button are in [My subscriptions](/help/student-subscriptions).

Next to every group you'll find:

- The **teacher's** name
- Its **times** — days of the week, start and end
- The **centre** — or "no centre" if the teacher works independently
- The **number of students** and the cap, if the group has one

## Two ways to subscribe

A group may offer one of these or both — that's up to the teacher or the centre:

### Monthly subscription

A **Subscribe monthly** button with the price on it. It covers the whole month's lessons.

1. Press the button
2. Complete the payment
3. Once it goes through, the group appears in [your schedule](/help/student-home-and-schedule)

> A monthly subscription **does not renew itself**, and nobody charges you without your knowledge. When the month ends you pay again yourself if you want to continue.

### Booking a single lesson

For one-off lessons. Next to the button is a **lesson picker**:

1. Open the picker and choose the **lesson** by date and time
2. Press **Book session**, which shows the per-lesson price
3. Complete the payment

**You must pick the lesson first.** If you press the button without choosing one, you'll get a message asking you to pick a lesson and no payment happens.

### A group with no prices

Some groups don't have prices set yet. Those show the price and a **Request to join** button instead of pay buttons. Press it and the request goes to the teacher or centre, who reply to you.

## Paying

Pressing any subscribe button takes you to the payment page to pay by card or mobile wallet. Once payment completes:

- Your subscription is **active** immediately
- Your lessons appear in your schedule
- You get a numbered [receipt](/help/student-receipts)

**Got money in your Edura wallet?** You can pay from it instead of a card — see [Wallet](/help/student-wallet).

**Not paying online?** You can go to the centre and pay cash at the counter; the staff record it and give you a receipt. Exactly the same result.

## Things you might run into

**"No groups in this subject"** — nobody has opened one near you yet. Try the **Browse all** tab, or ask your centre.

**The "My subjects" tab is empty** — you haven't chosen your grade and track. Finish [Choose your system, grade and track](/help/student-onboarding-track).

**The payment was declined** — you'll see a **"payment declined"** message and nothing is taken from you. Check the card details and try again, or pay from your [wallet](/help/student-wallet) or in cash at the centre.

**The group is full** — the count is shown next to it. Pick another group for the same subject.
