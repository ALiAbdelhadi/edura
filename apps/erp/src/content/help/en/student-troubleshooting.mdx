If something isn't working, look for it here first.

## Signing in and your account

### You forgot your password

1. On the sign-in page press **Forgot password**
2. Enter your email and send
3. Open the link you receive and set a new password

Can't find the message? Check your **spam** folder, and make sure you used the same email your account is registered with.

### "Email or phone already in use"

You already have an account. [Sign in](/login) instead of registering, and use **Forgot password** if you can't remember it.

### The invite link doesn't work

The link opens **once**. If you already used it, sign in normally with your email and password. If you haven't used it and it says invalid, ask the centre for a new one. [More detail](/help/student-claim-invite).

### You were signed out suddenly

The password was changed, or someone used **Sign out all other devices**. Sign in with the new password — and if you didn't change it, [change your password](/help/student-settings-security) immediately.

## Subjects and lessons

### The Subjects screen is empty

You haven't finished choosing your grade. Open [the wizard](/help/student-onboarding-track) and complete it.

### Your schedule is empty

You haven't joined a group, or your subscription ended. Go to [Subjects](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe).

### You can't find a particular teacher

Try the **Browse all** tab and search by name. If they're still not there, they haven't opened a group on Edura yet.

### The group says it's full

It's reached its cap. Pick another group for the same subject, or ask the centre whether they'll open a new one.

## Money

### You paid and the subscription didn't activate

1. Open your [Wallet](/help/student-wallet) and look at the **Receipts** section
2. **Is there a receipt?** Then the payment went through — refresh, and if it's still not active contact the centre with the receipt number
3. **No receipt?** The payment didn't complete and nothing was taken. Try again

### The payment was declined

Nothing is taken from you. Check the card details and its balance, or pay from your [wallet](/help/student-wallet), or pay cash at the centre.

### You topped up and the balance didn't rise

Wait a minute and refresh. If it's still the same, check your receipts: no receipt means nothing was charged.

### Paying from the wallet was refused

Your balance is short. Wallet payments are **all or nothing**, so no part of it is taken. Top up the difference and try again.

## Attendance and grades

### You attended but it's marked absent

Most likely your group uses **two check-ins** and you only checked in at the counter, not at the hall door. If that's not it, contact your teacher with the **lesson's date** — they're the one who can correct it. [More detail](/help/student-attendance).

### Your grades aren't showing

Your teacher hasn't recorded them yet. It's something they do; it isn't automatic.

## Card and messages

### The card won't appear

Your record is incomplete. The screen tells you exactly what's missing — complete it and press **Show my card**. [More detail](/help/student-id-card).

### You lost your card

Contact the centre or support. The old card is cancelled and a new one issued, and **your code doesn't change**.

### You can't message your teacher

Most likely you're under 18 and there's no **guardian email** on file. Add it in [Settings](/help/student-settings-security) and messaging unlocks immediately. A phone number alone isn't enough. [More detail](/help/student-messages).

### Someone is bothering you in messages

Open the conversation, press **Block** to stop them, then **Report** so the team sees the problem. If that person is an adult, **tell your parent as well**.

## Still stuck?

Talk to your centre — they're closest to you. If the problem is with your account itself or with money, contact support and **have your receipt number or the lesson date ready**, so you get an answer faster.
