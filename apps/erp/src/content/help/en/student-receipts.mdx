Every payment you make in Edura — online or cash at the centre — produces a **numbered receipt**. It's your proof that you paid, so keep it.

<Shot src="/help/student-receipts-en.webp" caption="The receipts list at the bottom of the wallet screen" />

## Where your receipts are

Open [**Wallet**](/help/student-wallet) and tap the **Receipts** tab at the top. Each line shows:

- The payment **date**
- The **receipt number**
- The **amount**
- A **Download** button that opens the full receipt

## What's on a receipt

- The **receipt number** and **date**
- **Your name** and your **student code**
- The **payer** — you, or your parent if they paid
- The **group or subject** the payment was for
- The **issuer** — the centre or the teacher
- **Who recorded the payment**, if it was cash at the counter
- The **total** and its state: **paid** or **refunded**

At the bottom it says the receipt is **system-generated** — produced automatically, not written by hand.

## Printing it or saving a PDF

1. Press **Download** next to the receipt
2. On the receipt page press the **Print** button
3. In the print dialog choose a printer as usual, **or choose "Save as PDF"** to keep it on your device and send it over WhatsApp

## Refund receipts

If money comes back to you, a receipt headed **Refund receipt** is produced showing the amount returned. The original payment receipt **stays where it is** and is never deleted — so what you paid and what came back both remain clear.

## Things you might run into

**The receipts section is empty** — you haven't paid for anything yet.

**You paid and there's no receipt** — the payment didn't complete, and nothing was taken. Check your [wallet](/help/student-wallet) and try again.

**"Page not found" when opening a receipt** — that receipt isn't yours, or the link is incomplete. Open it from the receipts list in your wallet rather than typing the link.

**Something on the receipt is wrong** — contact the centre and **give them the receipt number**. Details aren't edited in place; a correction is issued as a new receipt and the old one stays visible.
