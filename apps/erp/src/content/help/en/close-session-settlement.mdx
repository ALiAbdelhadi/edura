---
title: Closing a session and how the money is split
summary: Distribution happens when the session closes, for attending students only, and exactly once however many times it's closed.
---

Closing a session is not an administrative formality — **it is the moment the money moves**.

## Who closes a session

The teacher, from the **Close the lesson** card under the register, or the centre if it owns the group. An assistant can do it on the teacher's behalf, and the action is recorded under their name.

<Shot caption="The Close the lesson card under the register" />

## What happens on close

1. The system collects the students marked **present** for that session.
2. The per-session price is derived from the class price divided by its number of sessions.
3. For each present student, entries are written: the payment entry plus the commission entries.
4. The teacher's and the center's wallets are updated in the same moment.

All of it in **one transaction**: either everything lands, or nothing does.

## Only present students count

- Absent student → not counted
- Counter-only check-in in two-stage mode → **not counted**
- A session nobody attended → no distribution at all, and no commission

## Closing twice does not pay twice

If a session is already closed and someone closes it again, **nothing is distributed a second time**. The session records that it has been distributed, and the second attempt returns without writing an entry. That is guaranteed in the database, not by the button.

## One exception: the independent teacher

If the class belongs to no center (a teacher teaching on their own), money settles **at payment time**, not at close. Closing such a session only changes its state — it never pays again, and that is the guard against a double payment.
