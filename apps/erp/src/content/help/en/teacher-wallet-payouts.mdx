Your wallet moves when **you close a lesson**, not when a student pays. A student may have paid a week ago; if the lesson was never closed, your share has not landed.

<Shot caption="The wallet screen: balance, reserved, and available" />

## Balance is not the same as available

These are two different numbers, and the gap is not a fault:

- **Wallet balance** — everything credited to you so far.
- **Reserved by open requests** — amounts you have asked to withdraw that the accountant has not actioned yet.
- **Available** — balance minus reserved. This is what you can ask for right now.

So if you request 2,000 EGP, the balance does not drop — but the available figure drops by 2,000, so you cannot ask for the same money twice.

## Requesting a payout

1. Tap **Wallet** in the bar.
2. Press **Withdraw**.
3. Type an amount, or leave it empty to take everything available.
4. The button names the exact amount. Press it once — it waits and asks you to confirm.
5. Press again to send.

## Why two presses?

Because **a request cannot be cancelled once sent**. There is no button that withdraws it, on your screen or anywhere else — the only way back is for the accountant to reject it. The second press is your last chance to check the amount.

It is the same ceremony as closing a lesson, for the same reason: things that cannot be undone take two presses.

## Request status

- **Pending** — with the accountant, not yet actioned.
- **Approved** — accepted, transfer on the way.
- **Paid** — the money has been sent.
- **Rejected** — the reason is shown beside the request.

## Transactions

Under the requests you will find **recent wallet activity**: each line names its type (lesson earnings, commission, payout), the signed amount, and a reference number. Press **Show more** to go further back.

If a figure does not make sense, the reference (TXN-…) is what to quote when you ask us.
