Your wallet is your balance inside Edura. You top it up once, then pay for subscriptions from it without entering card details each time — which helps a lot if you're using a family member's card.

From **Wallet** in the menu.

<Shot src="/help/student-wallet-en.webp" caption="The wallet screen — balance and transaction history" />

## Your balance

At the top is a single large number: **wallet balance**. That number is **worked out from the record below it** — every piastre in it has a line you can look at, rather than a figure written on top.

## How to top up

1. Press the **Top up** button at the top
2. Enter the **Amount (EGP)**
3. Confirm and complete the payment by card or mobile wallet
4. As soon as it goes through the balance rises and a new line appears in the record

**The amount has to be a number greater than zero.** If you type something invalid the button stays off and you'll see **"enter a valid amount"**.

## How to pay from the wallet

Not from here — you pay from it at the moment you subscribe. On the [Subjects screen](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe), when you pick a group, choose to pay **from the wallet** instead of a card.

Paying from the wallet is **all or nothing**: if your balance is short, the whole thing is refused and no part of it is taken. Top up the difference and try again.

## Transaction history

Under the balance are two tabs: **Transactions** and **Receipts**. Transactions is the record of your wallet money, and each line shows:

- **Date** — The day of the movement
- **Type** — Top-up / payment / refund / adjustment
- **Reference** — The transaction number — quote it to the centre if you have a query
- **Amount** — Positive if in, negative if out
- **Balance after** — Your balance after that movement

> On a phone each movement is a full row rather than a table, so the amount and the balance are never squeezed off the screen.

### Types of movement

- **Top-up** — money came into your wallet.
- **Payment** — money left it for a subscription.
- **Refund** — money came back to you (a cancelled lesson, for example).
- **Adjustment** — a correction by platform staff, with a recorded reason.

### The record is never erased

Anything that enters this record stays in it forever. Even if there's a mistake, it's corrected with a **new line** and the wrong line stays visible — so you can always check your own money.

## Things you might run into

**"No wallet activity yet"** — you haven't topped up or paid yet. Press **Top up**.

**The record is long** — the page opens on the most recent 15, with a **Show all** button under them. Everything is already on your device, so that button loads nothing new — it just expands the list.

**The balance doesn't match** — if you see a warning about this, **don't pay from the wallet**; contact the centre or support with the reference. The record is always the authoritative version.

**You paid and the balance didn't rise** — wait a minute and refresh. If it's still the same, open the [**Receipts**](/help/student-receipts) tab: no receipt means the payment didn't complete and nothing was taken.
