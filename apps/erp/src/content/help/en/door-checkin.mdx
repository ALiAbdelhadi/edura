title: The second check-in at the hall door
summary: When to use two-stage mode, who scans at the door, and what happens to a student who stopped at the counter.
---

Some teachers want proof that whoever paid at the counter actually walked into the hall. That is what **two-stage mode** is for.

## The mode is per teacher

This setting belongs to the teacher, not the center. Each teacher chooses from their own settings:

- **Single check-in (default)** — the counter check-in is the attendance
- **Two-stage** — counter, plus a second check-in at the hall door

So you can have teachers on both modes in the same center, and that's fine.

## Who checks in at the door

The **teacher's assistant** handles the hall door, with the teacher themselves as the fallback. Counter staff do not check students in at the door — the whole point is a second confirmation from a second party.

## What happens in practice

1. The student checks in at the counter → state: **counter only**
2. The student reaches the hall door and the assistant checks them in → state: **present**

Anyone who stopped at step one stays visible to the teacher in a distinct, obvious state — not hidden, and not counted as attendance.

## The effect on money

**Only present students count.** A student in the "counter only" state gets no distribution when the session is closed. That's deliberate: if they never entered the hall, the lesson wasn't delivered to them.

> A duplicate check-in does not create a second record. If the assistant scans the same student twice, the result is the same.

## And if the connection drops at the door?

See [attendance with no connection](/help/offline-attendance) — check-in works offline and syncs afterwards.
