When you sign in, Edura doesn't show you a wall of numbers — it answers one question: **what's next?**

<Shot src="/help/student-home-and-schedule-en.webp" caption="The student home — the next session with its hall and teacher" />

## Home

This page opens on its own when you sign in, and **Home** takes you back to it.

### 1. Up next

One session at the top — your nearest one — carrying:

- **the group's name**
- **the day and time** — written the way you'd say it: "4:00 PM", not "16:00"
- **the hall** — where to go inside the centre
- **the teacher**

If it is running right now you'll see a **Now** mark beside it. If you have more sessions today, a line underneath says **"and 2 more sessions today"** and takes you to your schedule.

If it says **"No sessions coming up"**, you haven't subscribed to a group yet, or your group has no times set. Go to [Subjects](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe).

> **"Not set yet"** where the hall or the teacher should be means the centre hasn't decided yet. That's normal early in a term.

### 2. Your subscription notice

If a subscription is **ending soon** or **ended** recently, a notice appears here with a renew button. It only shows when something actually needs your attention, and it clears itself once you renew. See [My subscriptions](/help/student-subscriptions).

### 3. The guardian email notice

Sometimes you'll see a notice asking for **your guardian's email**. It isn't a formality — without it, messaging teachers and the centre stays locked for you. See [Messages](/help/student-messages) and finish it in [Settings and security](/help/student-settings-security).

### 4. Latest grade

The most recent assessment marked for you: your score out of the total, the percentage, the band, and the assessment's name and date. Tap it for [Grades](/help/student-grades). If nothing has been marked yet, this card doesn't appear at all.

### 5. Your record — three figures

Three boxes side by side. Each one is **tappable** and opens the entries behind it:

- **Attendance** — how much of your sessions you've attended — [Attendance](/help/student-attendance)
- **Average** — your average across subjects — [Grades](/help/student-grades)
- **Wallet balance** — what you have now — [Wallet](/help/student-wallet)

A dash means there's no data yet — nothing recorded for you so far.

### 6. Two shortcuts

**My ID** — the card you show at the counter. **Subscribe to a subject** — the groups open to you.

## Your schedule

From **Schedule**.

### Moving between days

At the top is a **full week with its dates**: each day with its number in the month. A day that has a session carries a **dot**, so you can find your next one without opening every day in turn. The page opens on **today**.

The arrows move you to **last week** or **next week**, keeping you on the same weekday.

### The day's sessions

Each session is one row: the group's name, start and end time, the hall, and the teacher. Tap any of them for the full detail — the date, the subject, and the session's state.

### Session states

- **Scheduled** — hasn't happened yet; this is your next one.
- **Completed** — it happened and attendance was recorded.
- **Cancelled** — called off. You aren't charged for it.

## Things you might hit

**The day is empty** — nothing that day. Look for the dots under the rest of the week.

**The whole week is empty** — either no subscription is running, or your sessions are in another week. Try the next-week arrow, then read [Subjects](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe).
