When you sign in, Edura doesn't show you a wall of numbers — it answers one question: **what's next?**

<Shot src="/help/student-home-and-schedule-en.webp" caption="The student home screen — up next and today's classes" />

## Home

This page opens by itself when you sign in, and you can get back to it from **Home** in the menu.

### 1. Up next

At the top there's exactly one class — your nearest one, with the subject and the time. If there's nothing today it shows the first class of the coming week.

If it says **"no upcoming classes"**, you either haven't joined a group yet or your group has no times set. Go to [Subjects and subscribing](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe).

### 2. Today / this week

Below that are two boxes side by side: **today's** classes, and **this week's** with dates and times. The today box has a **Schedule** link to the full schedule.

### 3. Your record — three numbers

Three tiles, and each one is **tappable** and takes you to what's behind it:

- **Attendance rate** — How much of your classes you've attended — [Attendance](/help/student-attendance)
- **Average Grade** — Your average grade as a percentage — [Grades](/help/student-grades)
- **Wallet balance** — What's in your wallet right now — [Wallet](/help/student-wallet)

A dash instead of a number means there's no data yet — no attendance or grades recorded so far.

### 4. How you're doing per subject

At the bottom, each subject with your percentage and band. Press **All grades** for the detail.

### 5. The guardian email notice

Sometimes a notice at the top asks for **your guardian's email**. It isn't a formality — without it, messaging teachers and centre staff stays locked for you. Read [Messages](/help/student-messages), and complete it from [Settings and security](/help/student-settings-security).

## Schedule

From **Schedule** in the menu.

### Moving between days

The days of the week are along the top. Tap any day to see its classes. The page opens on **today** automatically.

### Seeing a lesson's details

Tap any class in the list and its details open: the full date, start and end time, the group name, the subject, the teacher, the room, and the lesson's status.

### Lesson statuses

- **Scheduled** — hasn't happened yet; this is a class to come.
- **Completed** — it happened and attendance was recorded.
- **Cancelled** — called off. You aren't charged for it.

A **"—"** where the room or teacher should be means the centre hasn't assigned one yet.

## Things you might run into

**The day is empty** — no classes that day, or your classes are on other days. Try the days either side.

**The whole schedule is empty** — you have no active subscription yet. See [Subjects and subscribing](/help/student-subjects-and-subscribe).
