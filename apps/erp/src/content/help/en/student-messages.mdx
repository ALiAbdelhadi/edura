From **Messages** in the menu. You can message your teacher or your centre if you have a question outside class.

<Shot src="/help/student-messages-en.webp" caption="The messages screen — conversation list and an open conversation" />

## Starting a conversation

1. Press **New conversation**
2. The list shows **only the people you're allowed to message** — your teachers, their assistants, and your centre's staff
3. Tap a name, type in the box at the bottom, and send

There's a **filter by role** above the list if there are a lot of names.

> This list never shows anyone you aren't allowed to message. If someone isn't in it, you can't reach them — and there's no other route that would let you.

## Why messaging might be locked for you

If you're under 18 and **there's no guardian email** on file, messaging adults (teachers, assistants and centre staff) is **locked**.

It isn't a punishment — it's so a parent can see how you communicate with adults. To unlock it:

1. On [Home](/help/student-home-and-schedule) or in [Settings](/help/student-settings-security) you'll find a notice asking for a guardian email
2. Enter it and save
3. Messaging unlocks immediately

> **A guardian phone number alone isn't enough** — it has to be an email.

**Everything else works normally** while you're locked: your classes, grades, attendance, wallet and card. Only adult messaging is affected, and your account is not suspended.

## Your parent may be able to read

If you're under 18 and your parent is linked to your account, they can **read** your conversations with adults — read only; they can't write and can't delete. And **a clear notice appears in the conversation** for you and for the other person.

Conversations with other students are **not included**.

## If someone bothers you

### Block them

Open the conversation, press **Block**, and confirm. Blocking stops it in both directions — they can't message you and you can't message them — and old messages are not deleted.

### Report

Press **Report**, choose the **category**, and write the **details**. The report goes to the platform team and is reviewed.

**If an adult is bothering you, report it and tell your parent.** Blocking stops the messages; reporting is what gets the problem to someone who can act.

## Things you might run into

**"No conversations"** — you haven't started one. Press **New conversation**.

**The list is empty when starting a new conversation** — either you haven't subscribed with any teacher, or messaging is locked because there's no guardian email.

**"Rate limited"** — you sent too many messages too quickly. Wait a moment and try again.

**"Connection lost"** — your connection dropped. Messages resume by themselves when it's back.

**A message that says it was removed** — it was deleted. The text itself is still kept by the platform in case of a problem, so nobody can say something harmful and then erase it.
