These are the two most important minutes of your day. The register decides **who counts in the distribution**, and closing is what **actually moves the money**.

## Picking the lesson

Tap **Attendance** in the bar. Every row names:

- the group
- the day, date and time
- the hall

Lessons are grouped into **Today** and **Earlier**, going back 14 days.

## "At the centre, not in the lesson"

If you work in **counter + door** mode, this can happen: a student pays, walks into the centre, and never reaches the hall. That state is called **"at the centre, not in the lesson"**, and it is:

- **not counted as present**
- **not included in the distribution** — so your share shrinks while they have paid

That is why it does not sit loose in the register. It appears in **its own box above the register**, with a count. Each student there has only two buttons: **present** or **absent**. As soon as you decide, the row drops into the ordinary register and the count falls.

The box empties as you work. If you do not see it, nobody is in that state.

## Marking the register

Everyone starts as **present**, because the exception is the absentee, not the other way round. Mark the absentees, and use **excused** where there is a reason.

You can also scan a student's card with **Scan card**; that marks them present and nothing else — it does not save on its own.

Then press **Save attendance**.

> If the connection drops, save anyway. It is stored on the device, a bar shows how many are waiting, and **Sync now** sends them when you are back online.

## Closing the lesson

Under the register is the **Close the lesson** card. This is what credits your share.

Before you press, it shows you:

- how many were present
- the lesson price per student
- the teacher, centre and platform shares — per student and in total

The button itself names the amount. Press once, then **final confirm**.

**The distribution is written once and cannot be reversed.**

## If it refuses to close

The card tells you why rather than leaving you to guess:

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| The group has no price | Set the price first — without one there is nothing to distribute |
| No students present | Take the register first |
| Already closed | It was distributed before, and never repeats |
| The lesson is cancelled | There are no takings to share |
| Teacher-direct group | Its takings are distributed at payment time, not at closing |

## Lessons that passed without closing

**Home** shows how many lessons are still not closed, and on the group page each one is marked **"Not closed yet"**. That is work you have done whose money has not landed.

> **Note:** editing attendance locks **7 days** after the lesson. If a lesson older than that never had attendance recorded at all, you will not be able to close it — talk to us in that case.
